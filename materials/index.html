Marketing materials menu

What we make, who it’s for, and the main versions we have. Client-specific copies (a named flyer, a Creston preview, a MarJo guide) are customizations of these types, not separate menu items.

Every new piece should be responsive HTML that holds up on a phone, a laptop, and a big screen, and should be handed over as a link. A PDF is optional, only when asked, and only after it matches the HTML.

What we already make

Status in plain words: live means approved and hosted; draft means built but waiting on you; held means do not share; gap means we don’t have this type yet.

Live

Employer guides

Longer educational pieces an owner or HR lead can keep. The live one today is Crossing 50 (ACA employer mandate). Under-20, 20–49, and a rebuilt COBRA guide are the gaps in this type.

Live

Education hubs

Tabbed HTML pages that introduce Integrity / Amaze to employers or employees. Employer and employee hubs are live. A single resources page, and the videos page, are still missing or held.

Live

One-pagers

Short leave-behinds: overview, payroll setup, tax FAQ, employee examples, paycheck and taxes. These exist for Integrity / Amaze. We don’t yet have renewal, mid-market, or ICHRA / QSEHRA one-pagers.

Draft

Sales decks

Meeting presentations, built as HTML that reflows on a phone. The Integrity / Amaze decks are in draft (including contrast fixes still to apply on a held copy). A standalone Amaze master is an open decision.

Live

Flyers

Short announcements for a single employer, often for open enrollment or a wellbeing challenge. Recent examples include the Perceptive raffle and quarterly-challenge flyers. Each new flyer is a client customization, not a new type.

Held / live mix

Explainer videos

Short films that explain Integrity / Amaze or how the monthly wellbeing benefit shows up in a paycheck. Some earlier cuts are hosted; the current intro and paycheck rebuilds (v5 / v22) stay held until you approve a version.

Live, still tuning

Landing pages

Michigan SEO pages along the ladder: individual / ACA, small group, mid-market. They are live, but traffic and form leads are still thin. A drafted Lead-Fix batch is waiting on your OK; that is a ship decision, not a new type.

Templates only

Compliance calendars

Year-at-a-glance lists by group size (under 20, 20–99, 100+). Templates exist. They should be published as HTML after the size guides, not as a first leave-behind.

Gap or on hold

Social, articles, case studies, newsletters

Social is planned (LinkedIn first, X as the daily companion) but has not launched. A 2027 cost-outlook article is drafted and held. There is no published case-study type (only a Davenport stub) and no newsletter.
