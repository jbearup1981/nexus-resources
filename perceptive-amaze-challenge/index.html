The Amaze Health quarterly challenge

Each quarter, pick one task in the Amaze Health app. Amaze is a wellbeing program that doesn’t replace your health plan or the doctor you love — it’s a monthly wellbeing benefit, and this challenge is a simple way to put it to work.

Complete a quarter’s task for a reward like an extra HSA deposit. Reward amounts are still to be decided.

Quarter 1

Shop and ask

  • Use Amaze to help shop for a service paid with HSA money
  • Compare costs before a procedure or test
  • Or message an Amaze provider with a health question
Quarter 2

Get care on the calendar

  • Use Amaze to get an annual physical or checkup on the calendar
  • Find an in-network doctor or dentist
  • Or schedule a preventive screening
Quarter 3

Sort out a bill or claim

  • Have Amaze help negotiate an old medical bill
  • Review a bill or explanation of benefits
  • Or sort out a claim issue
Quarter 4

Save on care and plan ahead

  • Use Amaze before heading to urgent care
  • Find a lower-cost prescription option
  • Or plan next year’s HSA spending
Year-end
Bigger reward

Complete all four quarters

Finish a task in each quarter for a bigger reward, such as an extra PTO day.

This flyer is a proposal for later in 2027. Quarterly reward amounts have not been set. Amaze Health is a wellbeing program. It doesn’t replace your health plan or the doctor you love. It’s a monthly wellbeing benefit available to Perceptive Controls employees.
