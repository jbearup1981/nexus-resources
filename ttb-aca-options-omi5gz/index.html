The two questions the IRS asks

A · "Did you offer coverage to at least 95% of full-timers?"

If not, and even one full-timer gets a subsidized Exchange plan, the penalty is charged on all full-timers minus 30.

$3,780

per full-timer per year, 2027 ($315/mo)

What is minimum essential coverage (MEC)?

Any employer group health plan counts as MEC. A MEC-only plan (preventive-care focused) satisfies the A test but usually not the B test. IRS definition

B · "Was it affordable and minimum value?"

If not, the penalty is charged only for each full-timer who actually gets an Exchange subsidy, capped at what A would have been.

$5,670

per subsidized full-timer per year, 2027 ($472.50/mo)

What is minimum value (MV)?

The plan pays at least 60% of total allowed costs of covered services and includes substantial coverage of inpatient hospital and physician services. IRS Notice 2014-69

Offering protects you from the big one. Affordable + solid protects you from the small one.
Employees who enroll in Medicaid instead don't trigger a penalty. Part-timers can't trigger one either.

Penalty amounts: IRS Rev. Proc. 2026-22 (2027 amounts), as cited in the Nexus "Crossing 50" ACA guide, §3 (penalties table). Triggers and Medicaid: IRS Q&A on employer shared responsibility, cited in the same guide.

Do your other businesses count?

For the 50 test, the IRS treats related companies as one employer. Companies are tied together in three ways:

Parent–subsidiary

One company owns at least 80% of another (by vote or value; profits or capital for an LLC or partnership).

Brother–sister

The same 5 or fewer people own at least 80% of each company, and more than 50% when you count each person's smallest stake across the companies.

Affiliated service group

Service businesses that share owners and regularly work for or with each other (IRC §414(m)). Very fact-specific.

Whose shares count as yours: your spouse's (limited exceptions), your children under 21, options you hold, and your share of anything owned through a partnership, corporation, trust or estate. If you own more than 50% of a company, your parents', grandparents', grandchildren's and adult children's stakes count too.
If the combined count is 50+, every company is an ALE. Each company's penalty is figured on its own full-timers, and the 30 is split among the companies by full-timers.
What we need from you: a list of each business, every owner and their %, and full-time and part-time headcount for each.

Sources: IRC §414(b), (c), (m), applied to the ACA by 26 CFR 54.4980H-1(a)(16); 80% and more-than-50% tests: 26 CFR 1.414(c)-2; attribution: 26 CFR 1.414(c)-4; penalty per company and split of the 30: 26 CFR 54.4980H-4(a), (e); affiliated service groups: IRC §414(m). What "affordable" means for you

An employee's share for employee-only coverage can't be more than 10.22% of pay (2027 plan years). Only employee-only is tested, not family.

Poverty-line safe harbor

≤ $135.92/mo

Same number for everyone. No tracking. The safest test.

$15,960 × 10.22% ÷ 12

Wage-based (rate of pay / W-2)

≈ $306/mo

At your ~$3,000/mo average full-time pay: 10.22% × $3,000 = $306.60.

But it has to hold for each employee's own pay, so it takes tracking.

Why $135 looks low: it's tied to the federal poverty line, not to what your people earn.

10.22%: IRS Rev. Proc. 2026-26; $135.92 and $15,960 (2026 HHS poverty guideline): Nexus "Crossing 50" ACA guide, §4. $3,000/mo average full-time pay: John's email of 10/2/2026.

The West Michigan market

Two main carriers

Blue Cross Blue Shield of Michigan / BCN and Priority Health.

Fully insured vs. level funded

Fully insured: fixed premium, carrier keeps the risk.

Level funded: fixed monthly cost with a potential surplus back. Uses health underwriting, so it fits a healthier, stable group.

Large group, not small group

At 51+ you're rated as a large group: the carrier prices the whole group on age and gender mix, industry, location, and, as groups get bigger, claims. Typically more expensive than the small-group platform.

Typical single higher-deductible plan: roughly $500–$700/month

Fast lane vs. slow lane

A simple way to picture level funding vs. fully insured.

Fast lane · level funded / partially self-funded

Slow lane · fully insured

If a group doesn't qualify, it stays in the slow lane, and there's not a lot anyone can do about it.

ACA employer mandate

  • Offer coverage to at least 95% of full-timers (and their children under 26)
  • Affordability and minimum value on the lowest-cost employee-only plan
  • Track hours and full-time status every month
  • Forms 1094-C / 1095-C every year, even at $0 penalty; e-file at 10+ returns (W-2s count)

ERISA & plan documents

  • Plan document / wrap and Summary Plan Description (SPD)

  • Section 125 plan document for pre-tax premiums

Required notices

  • Summary of Benefits and Coverage (SBC)
  • Medicare Part D creditable coverage notice + CMS disclosure

Other federal items
