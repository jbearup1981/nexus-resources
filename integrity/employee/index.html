For employees · 1 of 3Integrity Health well-being program

Care for you and your family, right from your phone

Your employer offers Integrity Health, a well-being program with virtual care through Amaze. It works alongside your health plan and your own doctor.

Download PDF

01 Care when you need it

  • Unlimited virtual visits through Amaze for urgent, primary and specialist care (like dermatology), usually with no copay
  • Mental health support, from someone to talk to all the way to psychiatry
  • A care team for ongoing conditions like joint pain or diabetes
  • Help with the hard parts: referrals, prior authorizations and billing questions

Your family can use Amaze too.

02 Prescriptions and extras

  • Rx Valet card: 1,000+ commonly prescribed medications at $0 copay
  • Use it at 70,000+ retail pharmacies, with discounts on other drugs and help with medications over $200
  • No impact on your medical plan claims
  • Virtual pet care with licensed vets (not for emergencies) and a biometric facial scan that checks vital signs

03 Accident coverage for you

If you're hurt in a covered accident, the coverage pays set dollar amounts for events like an emergency room visit, a hospital stay or a broken bone. Amounts depend on your plan. For example, emergency room treatment pays $100 to $650 depending on plan. Accident coverage is for the employee only.

04 How it shows up in your paycheck

  • You're placed in one of six plans based on your pay and deductions
  • A pre-tax premium comes out of your paycheck through the Section 125 plan
  • As an enrolled member, you receive a monthly well-being benefit through payroll

05 Enrollment

  • If you qualify, you're enrolled automatically. You can choose to opt out.
  • Amaze sends you health education by email each week
  • New hires get an introduction from Amaze, then are enrolled on their next payroll unless they opt out
  • Employees age 70 and older can't enroll

This program doesn't replace your health plan or the doctor you love.
