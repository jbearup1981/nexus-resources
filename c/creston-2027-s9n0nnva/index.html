07 — Common Questions
What your CFO will ask.
How do we get employees to enroll?
Passive enrollment — the same model that took 401(k) participation from ~30% to over 80%. Employees are enrolled by default with a long, well-communicated runway to opt out before anything hits their paycheck.
How much work is this for payroll?
One new pre-tax deduction code and your standard deduction file. We handle communications, enrollment, opt-outs, eligibility and employee questions. Most groups are operational in 30–45 days with no HRIS rebuild.
Does this replace our health plan?
No. It sits alongside it. No change to your carrier, network, plan design or renewal cycle. Employees who waive your medical plan can still participate — and the program works for groups with no group medical at all.
Is the structure legitimate?
It rests on long-standing frameworks: Section 125 (in the code since 1978) and HIPAA excepted benefits. You receive a plan document, SPD, and ACA/ERISA notices.
Do employees do anything monthly?
Yes — about two minutes. The carrier needs a qualifying health interaction each month for the claim to issue: a telehealth visit, a short well-being check-in, or any covered service. Miss a month and there's simply no payment that month — no penalty, no clawback.
